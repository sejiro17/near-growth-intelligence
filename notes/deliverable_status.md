## Completion criteria

- **1. Supply reconciliation:** Eight weekly identities with independent issuance, actual burn and verified adjustments, canonical coverage and exact arithmetic.
- **2. Revenue/buyback ledger:** Historically attributed accounts/assets, eight-week reconciled opening/closing balances and classified fees, principal, settlements, refunds and verified purchases.
- **3. Persistent activity:** Several starting-week activity cohorts, exact +1/+4/+8 checkpoints wherever follow-up is complete, transaction contribution, concentration and scoped outcomes/spending.
- **4. Attribution/execution:** Reproducible population attribution and descendant coverage with historical roles, submitter/inner actor distinctions and no join duplication.
- **5. Conditional investment model:** Editable assumptions, units/formulas, disjoint revenue/destruction/custody outputs, thresholds, sensitivity and measurable revision conditions.
